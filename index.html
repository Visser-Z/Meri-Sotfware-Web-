Engineering workshop

Quoting & job tracking

For a gear and engineering workshop. Each bespoke job is quoted in the app and followed through the shop by stage, so the floor and the office work from the same picture and nothing sits forgotten on a bench.

Fits: fabricators, machine builders, equipment makers, repair shops.

Mining haulage

Tonnage reconciliation & fleet view

For a coal-haulage operation. Weighbridge tickets and delivery dockets are captured as they come in, tonnage hauled is reconciled against tonnage billed, and the fleet is visible in one place.

Fits: bulk transport, cross-border logistics, quarrying.

Fits: construction, EPC, mining, any regulated, document-heavy operation.
