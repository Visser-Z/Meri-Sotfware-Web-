Meri
Fruits.

The back office for fresh-produce consignment. Every morning it pulls the market's sales and payment reports, keeps your book, matches every payment, tracks every carton from your own delivery notes, and plans where next week's stock should go.

  • 01Pulls the market's reports every morning at 08:00
  • 02Matches every payment to its sales, to the cent
  • 03Checks what's owed against the market's own summary
  • 04Payments per delivery note: paid, market's cut, nett
  • 05Stock from your own delivery notes, with stocktake sheets
  • 06Buy plan split across the markets that sell it best
  • 07Paste the supplier's weekly stock, get a dispatch sheet
  • 08Ask questions in plain English, answered from your data

Built on one rule: a missing figure is never made up. Where a report doesn't carry a number, Meri Fruits asks for it once or flags the gap.
