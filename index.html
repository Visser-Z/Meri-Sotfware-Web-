MURR
App.

The back office for fresh-produce consignment. Produce goes to market agents on consignment and all you get back is their reports. MURR reads those reports, updates your own account-sales workbook, matches sales to payments and works out what each supplier is owed.

  • 01Reads market-agent sales, statement & payment reports
  • 02Updates your existing Excel book, formulas kept intact
  • 03Matches every sale to its payment run
  • 04Supplier settlement: earned, owed & paid
  • 05Flags unusual agent deductions, with the figures
  • 06Sell-through, days to sell & product rankings
  • 07Shows which product lines are worth taking on
  • 08Ask questions in plain English, answered from your data

Built on one rule: a missing figure is never made up. Where a report doesn't carry a number, MURR asks for it once or flags the gap.
