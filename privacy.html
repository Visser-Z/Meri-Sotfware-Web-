2. What we collect

Information you give us. When you use the intro call form or email us, we collect:

  • your name and email address (required);
  • your company name, phone or WhatsApp number (optional);
  • the services you're interested in, your message, and your preferred call time (optional).

Please don't send us special personal information (such as health, religious or biometric information) or information about children through the website.

Technical information. Like most websites, our hosting provider and the services we use may automatically receive your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit, as part of delivering the website. We do not use this to identify you.

3. Why we use it

We use your information only to:

  • respond to your enquiry and arrange an intro call;
  • understand what you need so the call is useful;
  • prepare a proposal or quote if you ask for one;
  • keep a record of our correspondence;
  • keep the website secure and prevent spam or abuse.

We process it because you have consented by submitting it, because it is needed to take steps you've asked for before a possible contract, and for our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries (POPIA section 11).

We will not send you direct marketing unless you have separately opted in, and you can opt out at any time.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with service providers ("operators") who help us run the website and our business, and only as far as needed:

  • Formspree, which receives and delivers intro call form submissions to us;
  • our email and hosting providers;
  • Google Fonts, which serves the website's fonts and receives your IP address when a page loads.

We may also disclose information where the law requires it, or to protect our rights in a legal matter.

5. Transfers outside South Africa

Some of these service providers, including Formspree and Google, store or process information outside South Africa (for example in the United States). We only use providers that are subject to laws, binding agreements or policies that give your information a level of protection substantially similar to POPIA, as required by section 72. By submitting the form, you consent to this transfer.

6. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry information for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and any resulting relationship, and generally for no longer than 24 months after our last contact, unless the law requires us to keep it longer or you become a client (in which case client records are kept as required by law). After that, it is deleted or de-identified.

7. How we protect it

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information against loss, misuse and unauthorised access, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections and access controls on our accounts. No internet transmission is completely secure, however, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

If we become aware of a security compromise affecting your personal information, we will notify you and the Information Regulator as required by POPIA section 22.

8. Cookies

This website does not set its own tracking or advertising cookies, and we do not use analytics that profile you. Third-party services listed in section 4 may use strictly necessary technical data to deliver their service.

9. Your rights

Under POPIA you have the right to:

  • ask whether we hold personal information about you, and request a copy;
  • ask us to correct, update or delete it;
  • object to us processing it, and withdraw consent at any time;
  • object to direct marketing;
  • lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator.

To use any of these rights, email VisserMeridian@outlook.com. We may need to verify your identity first. Withdrawing consent doesn't affect processing that happened before you withdrew it.
