Questions.

Is an AI employee a person?

No. Atlas, Nova and Echo are software. The names make it easy for your team to talk about who does what. Your customers and suppliers never see them: messages go out in your company's name, from your own address.

What does it cost?

It depends on the job, how much of it there is and which systems it works with, so we price each business after the free workflow review. You see what it saves you first, and the price is in writing before any work starts.

Will this replace my staff?

It takes the repetitive part of the job, so your people handle more without working longer. Decisions, complaints and anything unusual stay with your team.

Do we need new software?

No. AI employees work on top of what you run now: email, your accounting package and your TMS or ERP where it allows a connection. The workflow review checks what you have.

Can it pay suppliers or send money?

No. Nova prepares the payment pack and flags problems. A person approves and makes every payment.

What if it gets something wrong?

Anything an AI employee can't do with certainty goes to a person instead of being guessed. During the pilot your team checks its work, and we measure how often it needed help.

What about our data?

An AI employee only reads what its job needs. Our Privacy Notice has the details.
