Questions

Good questions.

How do I get customers to pay their invoices on time?

Remind them early and consistently, starting before the due date, and make paying easy by attaching the invoice and your banking details. The Credit Controller does exactly this on a schedule you set, and only for invoices the Reconciler has confirmed are unpaid.

What is the free money owed check?

Send us last month's bank statement and invoice list, and we run them through the Reconciler. You get back a report of what's been paid, what's still owed and which payments couldn't be matched, and we walk you through it on a short call. It's free and there's no obligation to sign up.

How much does Meri.Conquer cost?

Setup is R5,000 once off. After that you pay a monthly fee based on how much work the employees do, meaning the number of invoices and bank lines they handle. You get a fixed quote on the intro call.

Do I need Xero, Sage or Pastel to use it?

No. Your open invoices can come from an Excel sheet or an export from whatever accounting system you use. Bank statements come in as PDF or CSV.

Which banks does the Reconciler work with?

It reads the statements South African banks already give you, as PDF or CSV, including FNB, Absa, Standard Bank, Nedbank and Capitec. We set it up on your own statement format.

Will it send reminders to customers who have already paid?

No. The Credit Controller only chases invoices the Reconciler has confirmed unpaid that morning. Anything unclear, like a cash deposit with no reference, comes to you instead of triggering a reminder.

Is this a debt collection service?

No. It's your own credit control: reminders go out from your business, in your name, about your own invoices. We don't collect money on your behalf or hand debts over to attorneys.

Can it send reminders on WhatsApp?

Reminders go out by email as standard. WhatsApp reminders are available as an add on, sent from your business number using message templates approved by WhatsApp.
