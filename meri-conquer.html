Questions

Do I need accounting software?

No. If you use Xero, Meri checks payments and quotes there. If not, your bank statements are forwarded to Meri by email and it matches payments from those.

Will my customers know it's automated?

Messages come from your own email address, signed with your name, in the tone you choose. You can edit any message before it goes.

What if a customer has already paid?

Payments are checked every morning, and again right before a message is sent. If it's paid, nothing goes.

What if they reply?

Meri notices the reply, pauses the follow-ups to that customer and shows it to you. You tap won, lost, or keep going.

Is this debt collection?

No. It's your own follow-up: messages go from your business, in your name, about your own quotes and invoices. We never collect money on your behalf.

Is my information safe?

Meri only reads what it needs: your sent quotes and invoices, and your payments. Banking details are encrypted, and nothing is shared with anyone. See our Privacy Notice.

What does it cost?

Setup is R5,000 once off, then a monthly fee based on how much work Meri does for you. You get a fixed quote on a free setup call.
